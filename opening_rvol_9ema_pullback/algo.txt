• In plain English, it does this:

  The current code uses a research window from 2023-01-01 through 2026-08-25.

  1. Before the open, it builds a stock list of US companies with:

  - market cap above $5B
  - price between $30 and $500
  - positive trailing-12-month net income

  2. At 9:45 a.m. ET, it checks which of those stocks have strong opening volume:

  - RVOL = today’s first 15-minute volume
  - divided by the median first 15-minute volume from the previous 20 sessions
  - only stocks with RVOL >= 1.5 stay in play

  3. At 9:46 a.m. ET, those RVOL names become the live intraday universe.
  4. For each live stock, it builds 2-minute candles and tracks:

  - a 9 EMA
  - an ATR(14)

  5. The setup is valid only if the 2-minute candles have held above the 9 EMA since the open.

  - Wicks below the EMA are fine
  - any 2-minute close below the EMA invalidates the setup for the day

  6. If the first 15 minutes held, it waits for the first pullback after that where price:

  - touches the 9 EMA, or
  - comes within 0.5% of it

  7. On that pullback, it enters long.
  8. Position/risk rules:

  - 10% of portfolio per trade
  - keep 5% cash free at all times
  - initial stop-loss = entry price - 1.5 x ATR(14)
  - after entry, the stop trails higher as a chandelier stop:
  - highest price since entry - 2.5 x live ATR(14)
  - the stop only ratchets tighter; it never loosens intraday

  9. Exit rules:

  - initial ATR stop or trailing chandelier stop hit
  - 2-minute close below the 9 EMA after entry
  - no new entries after 11:30 a.m. ET
  - all remaining positions are sold 1 minute before the closing bell

  SPY is just used as the market-hours clock.
